🎶 SWAMP TUNES

The Discord music bot that plays YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud & Deezer in your voice channel.

➕ Add to Discord

Where the music comes from

▶️ YouTubeSearch any song or paste a link — direct audio.
🟢 SpotifyTrack, album & playlist links — matched automatically.
🟠 SoundCloudPaste a link, or search with sc:.
🟣 DeezerTrack, album & playlist links — no setup needed.

Commands

/play — play a song or link, or queue a whole playlist
/stop — stop playback & clear the queue
/disconnect — leave the voice channel
/volume — set volume 0–200
/bassboost — toggle bass boost on/off
/pause — pause / resume playback

Get it in your server

  1. Hit Add to Discord above and pick your server.
  2. Join a voice channel.
  3. Type /play never gonna give you up — or paste a Spotify/YouTube/SoundCloud/Deezer link.
